The old rules haven't worked

Almost everything you've been taught about finding love is backwards.

You've tried it. Maybe for years. And maybe it brought you people who liked the smaller version of you, or people who couldn't love anyone at all.

The old advice:

  • Be a little less.
  • Less eager.
  • Less sensitive.
  • Less intense.
  • Less you.

There is another way. It's the only one I've ever seen work.— Ken

The heart of Deeper Dating®

You are already magnetic. You've been hiding the magnet.

Forty years as a psychotherapist taught me one thing above all: the parts of you that you hide are the parts the right person falls in love with.

Your sensitivity. Your intensity. Your big, tender heart. The things you've been told are “too much.”

I call these your Core Gifts.

When you lead with your Core Gifts, the people who can love you well begin to see you. People listen more. People notice you more. And you lose interest in the ones who can't love you well.

You can't fake this kind of magnetism. It's already yours.

Leads to pain

Attractions of deprivation

Leads to love

Attractions of inspiration

Where the person you're with gets you, sees you, treasures you, protects you and takes care of you, and loves what they see when they see your Core Gifts.

You don't have to do this alone

So why hasn't it happened yet? Because this is scary.

Showing someone the real you is one of the bravest things you can do in love. Most of us can't do it alone.

I couldn't. I was a psychotherapist. I had plenty of insight. And I was single for so many years.

Understanding a pattern doesn't change it. Practice changes it. A guide who knows the way. People beside you who get it.
